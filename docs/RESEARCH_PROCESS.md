# Lead Research Process

Standard methodology for deep-dive prospect research at LoreWeaver.

---

## When to Use

- High-fit leads (85+) before outreach
- Partnership exploration
- Pre-meeting prep
- Competitive intelligence on specific studios

---

## Phase 1: Company Intel

### Basic Facts
- [ ] Legal name, founding year, location(s)
- [ ] Company size (employees, team structure)
- [ ] Funding history (if any)
- [ ] Public filings (Companies House UK, KvK NL, etc.)

### Sources
- Company website (About, Team, Careers)
- LinkedIn company page + employee count
- Crunchbase / PitchBook (funding)
- Government registries (financials for EU companies are often public)

---

## Phase 2: Key People

### Decision Makers
- [ ] Founders / C-suite
- [ ] Technical Director / Lead Engineer (tooling decisions)
- [ ] Narrative Director / Lead Writer (Architect user)
- [ ] Producer (budget holder)

### For Each Person
- [ ] Role and tenure
- [ ] LinkedIn profile
- [ ] Twitter/social presence
- [ ] Conference talks (GDC Vault, YouTube)
- [ ] Published articles/interviews

### Warm Path Analysis
- [ ] Mutual connections?
- [ ] Same conferences? (GDC, AdventureX, Nordic Game, Gamescom)
- [ ] Community overlap? (Discord servers, open source)

---

## Phase 3: Product & Tech Stack

### Games Analysis
- [ ] Released titles (last 5 years)
- [ ] Platforms (PC, console, mobile)
- [ ] Genre and narrative depth
- [ ] Reception (reviews, awards, sales if known)

### Technical Approach
- [ ] Engine (Unity, Unreal, custom)
- [ ] Narrative tools (ink, Yarn, Twine, proprietary)
- [ ] AI usage (any existing integration?)
- [ ] Open source contributions

### Sources
- Steam/store pages
- Technical postmortems (GDC, Gamasutra/GameDeveloper)
- GitHub repos
- Job postings (reveal tech stack)

---

## Phase 4: Pain Points & Opportunities

### Signals to Look For
- [ ] Hiring for narrative/tools roles (scaling pain)
- [ ] Blog posts about workflow challenges
- [ ] Conference talks about "what we learned"
- [ ] Community complaints (Reddit, Twitter, Discord)

### Timing Indicators
- [ ] Project phase (pre-prod, production, post-launch)
- [ ] Recent funding (budget available)
- [ ] Team growth (new hires = new processes)
- [ ] Upcoming releases (too busy vs. planning next)

---

## Phase 5: Competitive & Partnership Landscape

### Current Tool Usage
- [ ] What narrative tools do they use/build?
- [ ] Any AI tools in their pipeline?
- [ ] Middleware partners?

### Partnership Potential
- [ ] Complementary tech? (their tool + our AI)
- [ ] Distribution channel? (if they have a platform)
- [ ] Co-marketing? (shared audience)

### Competitive Position
- [ ] Could they build this themselves?
- [ ] Are competitors already talking to them?

---

## Phase 6: Outreach Strategy

### Angle Selection
Based on research, pick primary angle:
- **Pain point**: "We solve X problem you have"
- **Enhancement**: "Make your existing tool better"
- **Partnership**: "Together we can..."
- **Validation**: "Get your feedback on our approach"

### Contact Plan
- [ ] Who to contact first (and why)
- [ ] Warm intro possible?
- [ ] Cold outreach channel (email, Twitter DM, LinkedIn)
- [ ] Conference intercept opportunity?

### Messaging Draft
- [ ] Personalized hook (reference their work)
- [ ] Value prop (specific to their situation)
- [ ] Ask (meeting, demo, feedback call)

---

## Output Format

Research should produce:

1. **CRM Lead Update**
   - Updated notes with key findings
   - Contact info filled in
   - Tags updated (partnership-potential, ready-for-outreach, etc.)

2. **Research Doc** (for complex leads)
   - Save to `research/leads/[company-name].md`
   - Include sources for all claims
   - Date the research (info gets stale)

3. **Action Items**
   - Clear next steps
   - Owner assigned
   - Timeline

---

## Research Integrity Rules

From MEMORY.md Prime Directive:

> **ONLY include information that is VERIFIABLY TRUE.**
> - No made-up emails or guessed contacts
> - Every field must be traceable to a real source
> - If unknown, leave blank — don't fabricate

Always cite sources. Date your research. Flag uncertainty.

| Lead Priority | Research Depth | Time Budget |
|---------------|----------------|-------------|
| Exploratory | Phase 1-2 only | 15-30 min |
| Standard | Phase 1-4 | 1-2 hours |
| Deep Dive | All phases | 2-4 hours |
| Partnership | All + strategy | Half day |
