# Ideal Customer Profile: Architect

LoreWeaver Architect is a **narrative authoring tool** for game writers and narrative designers.

---

## Product Summary

**What it is:** SaaS tool for creating, organizing, and managing branching narrative content.

**What it's NOT:** Runtime AI that generates dialogue in-game.

**Pricing:** €10-200/seat/month (Solo → AAA tiers)

## AI Positioning

### 🔑 Critical Distinction: Production vs. Creative AI

**Architect = Production AI**
- Helps writers **organize and produce** content
- AI assists with tagging, consistency checking, lore tracking
- Human writes everything; AI helps manage it
- Think: "AI-powered writing assistant" not "AI writer"

**Director = Creative/Generative AI**
- Generates NPC dialogue at runtime
- AI creates content players experience
- This is what AI-skeptical studios fear

### Implications for Leads

| Studio AI Stance | Architect Fit | Director Fit |
|------------------|---------------|--------------|
| "AI for production, not creation" | ✅ PERFECT | ❌ Conflicts |
| Anti-AI / No generative AI | ✅ May accept | ❌ Hard no |
| AI-curious / Exploring | ✅ Good | ✅ Good |
| AI-forward / Using LLMs | ✅ Good | ✅ Good |

### Example: Remedy Entertainment

> "We're not using generative AI... using AI for the 'dull' parts of game development"

- ✅ **Architect fits**: Production tooling for writers
- ❌ **Director doesn't fit**: Runtime generation = what they explicitly reject

## Qualifying Questions

1. How do you currently manage branching narrative? (Spreadsheets = pain)
2. How many writers on your team? (2+ = collaboration pain)
3. Do you struggle with lore consistency? (Yes = Architect value)
4. What's your stance on AI in games? (Determines messaging)

---

## Messaging by AI Stance

### For AI-skeptical studios:
> "Architect is an authoring tool, not a content generator. Your writers create everything—we just help them stay organized. No AI writes your dialogue."

### For AI-curious studios:
> "Architect uses AI to help writers manage complexity—consistency checking, lore tracking, variant suggestions. The human is always in control."

### For AI-forward studios:
> "Architect integrates with your LLM workflows while keeping humans in the loop for quality control."

---

## Red Flags (Not a Fit)

- Solo dev who writes everything in plain text (too small)
- Studio with proprietary narrative tools they love (build vs buy)
- Mobile/casual games with minimal narrative
- Multiplayer-only studios with no story mode
