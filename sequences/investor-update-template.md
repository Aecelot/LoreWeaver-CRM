# Investor Update Template

**Format:** Quarterly PDF + email summary  
**Timing:** End of quarter (March, June, September, December)  
**Send via:** BCC to investor list

---

## Email Body (keep brief)

```
Subject: LoreWeaver – Investor Update Q[X] [YEAR]

Dear all,

Please find attached our Q[X] [YEAR] investor update. [1-2 sentence summary of the quarter's theme].

[2-3 sentences highlighting the biggest wins and what's next.]

If you have any questions, please let us know!

Thank you again for your confidence in LoreWeaver.
Kind regards,

-- 
Rijk Groenewoud
Founder & CEO | +31 6 1898 7973 | LinkedIn
www.loreweaver.ink
```

---

## PDF Structure (2-4 pages)

### Page 1: Highlights

**Quarter at a Glance**
- 3-5 bullet points of biggest wins
- Use metrics where possible

**Key Metrics**
| Metric | Last Quarter | This Quarter | Change |
|--------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| Architect users | X | Y | +Z% |
| MRR/Revenue | €X | €Y | +Z% |
| Team size | X | Y | +Z |
| LOIs signed | X | Y | +Z |

---

### Page 2: Product Progress

**Architect**
- Version updates
- Key features shipped
- Customer feedback/quotes

**Director**  
- Development milestones
- Technical achievements
- Demo/pilot status

---

### Page 3: Commercial & Team

**Traction**
- New LOIs/customers
- Pipeline highlights
- Key meetings/partnerships

**Team**
- New hires
- Advisors/contractors

---

### Page 4: Financials & Ask

**Runway**
- Current burn rate
- Months of runway
- Revenue trajectory

**Next Round**
- Target amount
- Timeline
- Use of funds

**How You Can Help**
- Specific asks (intros, advice, etc.)
