# Architect Cold Outreach Sequence

**Product:** LoreWeaver Architect (narrative design tool)
**Target:** Narrative leads, creative directors, producers at studios with branching dialogue
**Tone:** Peer-to-peer, casual, no corporate fluff
**Cadence:** 5 emails over 20 days

---

## Email 1 — Day 0 (First Touch)

**Subject:** `narrative production for {{company}}`

Hey {{firstName}},

I'm Rijk, founder of LoreWeaver. Saw {{trigger}} — {{company}} clearly takes narrative seriously.

We built Architect for studios like yours. It's an AI-native narrative production tool — not another authoring environment where you manually build dialogue trees, but a **production engine** that does the boring work so your writers can focus on the creative work.

**The concrete workflow:**

Import lore docs, character bibles, quest notes → Architect auto-structures it into usable narrative building blocks → auto-outlines quests → generates branching dialogue variants → exports engine-ready data. In seconds, not weeks.

**What this eliminates:**

- The "translation tax" between writer docs and game-ready structure
- Manual node plumbing, formatting, and five versions of the truth
- The export bottleneck (cleanup, reformatting, consistency checks)
- "Wait, which version is canon?" moments

**What stays human:**

Voice, tone, theme, character psychology, pacing, moral stakes — everything that makes narrative actually *matter*. Architect handles the scaffolding so your team gets more creative shots on goal.

We're working with EU studios right now who've cut their narrative ops overhead significantly. Would a 20-minute demo make sense?

— Rijk
rijk@loreweaver.ink

## A/B Test Ideas

**Subject lines to test:**
- `{{company}} + branching dialogue` vs `quick question about {{company}}'s narrative pipeline`
- `re:` prefix on follow-ups (yes/no)

**Email 1 variants:**
- Question-first vs observation-first
- With/without "Not asking for a call" line

---

## Disqualification Triggers

Stop sequence if:
- Studio has publicly stated "no AI tools" policy
- Contact replies with explicit no
- Company is < 5 people (not ICP)
- No narrative-heavy games in portfolio

---

## Notes

- **Architect = production AI**, not generative. Safe for studios wary of AI.
- Keep emails under 80 words — respect their inbox.
- One CTA per email. Never stack asks.
- Email 4 requires the guide to exist — create or link to blog post.
